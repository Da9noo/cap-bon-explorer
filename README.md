# Cap Bon Explorer

> A tourism website to explore the Cap Bon region (Nabeul, Tunisia).
> University project: all data, prices and contact details are fictional. No payment is made and no data is sent to a server.

## Quick Description

Cap Bon Explorer was built to make life easier for visitors of the Nabeul area. It helps them find the best activities in the region, book them online by filling in a simple form, and get in touch with us for any question or feedback about their trip.

## Technologies

Since this is a university project, we were limited to a few technologies, and we tried to get the best results out of them:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Bootstrap 5 (local use)

## How to Use the Website

The site was designed with a simple interface so that visitors can find their way easily.

1. **Home page:** discover Cap Bon, a selection of activities, and the reasons to visit the region.
2. **Activities page:** reach it with the "Voir les activités" button on an activity card or with the menu at the top. You will find all the activities, with a search bar (by title or place) and a filter by category. Each card has a "Voir les détails" button that opens the full description.
3. **Agenda page:** check the planned events for the next few months to organize your holidays better.
4. **Reservation page:** fill in a simple form with your personal information. The total price is calculated automatically, and if you come with 4 people or more you get a **10% discount**.
5. **Contact page:** find our email and phone number, read the FAQ to solve common questions, or send us a message with your feedback.
